📖 B.O.S. Rules

Moves are chosen secretly, then revealed together. A landed attack starts the Chopsticks interaction.

🖐️ Hands
Start at 1 | 1. Each hand holds 0–4.
💀 Hand death
5 wraps to 0. A 0 hand is dead.
🔫 Shoot
Choose your attacking hand and the opponent hand. Its value is the damage. 2-turn cooldown.
🛡️ Block
Stops Shoot and Wash. Max 3 consecutive uses. Any non-Block resets the streak.
🔄 Reflect
Reflects Shoot back at the attacking hand; counters Wash and Mind Control. 4-turn cooldown.
🧼 Wash
Resets your hands to 1 | 1. 8-turn cooldown.
🧠 Mind Control
Makes the opponent use a Shoot against themselves. V4 uses deterministic hand priority, not RNG. 4-turn cooldown.
🔀 Swap
Redistribute your own total between your hands. You choose the exact legal split. 5-turn cooldown.
🔒 Lock
Choose one opponent hand. It is unavailable for the next Sticks interaction. 3-turn cooldown in V4.
🥷 Steal
Choose an opponent hand and take exactly 1 finger from it. 5-turn cooldown in V4.

V4 bug fixes

  • Shoot target/turn handling is deterministic.
  • Swap is no longer RNG.
  • Steal always removes the stolen finger before adding it.
  • Mind Control no longer randomly selects hands.
  • Lock, Swap and Steal have cooldowns.
  • Cooldowns tick after completed War turns.

Spreadsheet note: the spreadsheet lists Lock as 5 turns and Steal as 7 turns, while George's playtest requested Lock 3 and Steal 5. V4 uses the playtest fixes requested for this build. The spreadsheet marks Sudden Death and detailed Swap/Lock/Steal targeting as proposed/TBD, so Sudden Death is not forced here.
